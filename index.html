Head Hunter

Every company on your list, with the jobs or HR route to use first: a jobs inbox, a hiring portal, or the general inbox when that's all they publish. Track who you've sent to as you go.

Your list

Start with any spreadsheet of companies, in any industry. Columns it understands: name (required), region, site, jobs, gen, portal, hiring, note. Excel files (.xlsx) and CSV both work.

Your letter

Write it once. {company}, {role} and {name} are filled in for each company when you press Draft email, which opens your own mail app. Attach your CV there before sending.

Everything is saved in this browser only. Export a CSV now and then to keep a backup.

Head Hunter keeps no server and sends nothing on its own: your list and statuses live in this browser, and emails open in your own mail app.
